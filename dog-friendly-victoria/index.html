Who we are

Independent. Single-issue. For dogs and the people who love them.

Dog Friendly Victoria is a proposed independent, single-issue political party focused on expanding responsible dog access across Victoria while protecting the freedom of businesses to choose.

Our vision

Dogs welcomed as part of everyday life.

A more dog-friendly Victoria where well-behaved dogs are welcomed as part of everyday life, and responsible dog owners can enjoy more public and commercial spaces with their companions.

Our core policy

Dog-friendly by default, rather than by exception.

Dogs would be permitted in eligible shops, cafés, restaurants, pubs and other commercial premises unless the business chooses to opt out or a statutory exemption applies. Businesses would retain the right to refuse entry without needing to justify their decision.

Welcome by default

Dogs permitted in eligible shops, cafés, restaurants, pubs and other commercial premises.

Businesses can opt out

Any business keeps the right to refuse entry — without needing to justify its decision.

Exemptions still apply

Where a statutory exemption applies, it continues to apply.

Our key objectives

Eight objectives. One balanced reform.

What we'd change

  • 01Establish a statewide dog-friendly-by-default framework.
  • 02Pursue changes to food safety regulations to permit dogs in indoor dining areas.
  • 03Introduce consistent, recognisable signage for businesses that choose to opt out.
  • 04Promote responsible dog ownership, training and appropriate behaviour in public spaces.

What we'd protect

  • 05Protect business owners' freedom of choice.
  • 06Maintain appropriate public health, hygiene and safety standards.
  • 07Protect existing assistance animal access rights.
  • 08Respect the needs of people with allergies, fears or other concerns about dogs.

Our principles

What guides us.

Inclusion

Recognising dogs as an important part of many Victorians' lives.

Freedom of choice

Respecting the decisions of businesses and individuals.

Responsibility

Holding dog owners accountable for their animals' behaviour.

Balance

Considering the needs of dog owners, businesses and the wider community.

Evidence-based reform

Supporting practical changes informed by research and consultation.

Our mission

To pursue legislative, regulatory and cultural change that makes Victoria more welcoming to dogs and their owners, without compromising public safety or individual choice.
