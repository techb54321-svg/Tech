Our key objectives

Eight goals. Tap one.

What we'd changeWhat we'd protect
1

Statewide framework

Establish a statewide dog-friendly-by-default framework.

2

Dogs in indoor dining

Pursue changes to food safety regulations to permit dogs in indoor dining areas.

3

Clear opt-out signage

Introduce consistent, recognisable signage for businesses that choose to opt out.

4

Responsible ownership

Promote responsible dog ownership, training and appropriate behaviour in public spaces.

5

Business freedom of choice

Protect business owners' freedom of choice.

6

Health, hygiene & safety

Maintain appropriate public health, hygiene and safety standards.

7

Assistance animal rights

Protect existing assistance animal access rights.

8

Respect for everyone

Respect the needs of people with allergies, fears or other concerns about dogs.

Our mission

Make Victoria more welcoming to dogs and their owners — without compromising public safety or individual choice.
