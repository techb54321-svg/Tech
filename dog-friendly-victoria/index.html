About us

Dogs, people and shared spaces.

Dog Friendly Victoria is a proposed independent advocacy organisation exploring changes to dog access in shops and hospitality venues. Rosie Bear is the inspiration for our visual identity.

Our proposed approach balances access for pet dogs with the needs of businesses, customers and the wider community.

Our proposed framework

Dog-friendly by default.

Eligible premises would welcome well-behaved pet dogs unless the business opts out or a legal exemption applies.

Questions and answers

What would change?

Would every shop have to accept dogs?

No. Eligible businesses could choose to remain dog-free, set reasonable conditions and ask owners to remove disruptive pets — without needing to justify the decision.

Can pet dogs already enter indoor cafés?

Generally, no. Under current food safety rules, a business can choose to allow pet dogs in outdoor dining areas, but not indoors. Indoor hospitality access would require relevant food safety reforms.

What about assistance animals?

Nothing would be taken away. Existing assistance animal access rights would remain protected.

Is Dog Friendly Victoria a registered political party?

No. Dog Friendly Victoria is a proposed independent advocacy organisation. We are not currently presented as a registered political party.

What about allergies and safety?

Businesses could stay dog-free. Dogs would remain on leads, under control and away from food preparation areas, and public health, hygiene and safety standards would be maintained. The needs of people with allergies, fears or other concerns about dogs would be respected.
