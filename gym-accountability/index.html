Fail-safes

1
Install this app. Open your browser menu and choose "Add to Home Screen" so it lives next to your other apps.
2
Put reminders in your calendar. A web app cannot wake your phone by itself, so your calendar does. The file below adds recurring alerts on your gym days plus a deadline check on the last day of each week.
3
Allow notifications for nudges while the app is open.
4
Back up the ledger so a lost phone is not a lost record. Export after each week and keep the file somewhere safe.
