Ironclad · referee invitation

Someone is asking you to be their gym referee.

It takes about two minutes a week. Read this, then decide.

What this is

They have made a commitment contract to go to the gym regularly. An app on their phone verifies each session with GPS at check-in and check-out, keeps a tamper-evident log, and reports the result every week. The app can count sessions, but it cannot make a missed week cost anything. That is what a referee is for: you are the person they answer to.

Your job

  • Read the weekly result. That is most of it.
  • If it says FAIL, collect the stake. Do not let it slide and do not accept excuses that were not declared in advance. The whole thing only works if a missed week actually costs something.
  • Treat silence as failure. If a week goes by with no result, or a message says no sessions were reported, assume the week was missed and ask.

You do not have to nag, track anything, or check in on them. Illness or travel can be declared in advance as a "freeze" week, and you will be told when that happens.

What you receive, and privacy

Not for you? Just tell them. No hard feelings, and they can ask someone else.
