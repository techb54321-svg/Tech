Ironclad · referee invitation

Someone wants you to hold them to something.

Two minutes a week. Read this, then decide.

What this is

They made a deal: 3 gym sessions a week, or it costs them. An app on their phone checks the GPS when they arrive and when they leave, insists on a minimum stay, and keeps a log nobody can edit. What the app can't do is make a missed week actually hurt.

That part is you.

Your job

  • Read the weekly verdict. That's most of it.
  • Treat silence as failure. No verdict, or a message saying the app reported nothing? Assume the week was missed and ask.

Not your job: nagging, tracking, policing. Illness or travel can be declared in advance as a "freeze" week, and you'll be told when that happens. Excuses that arrive after the fact don't count. That's the rule they chose, not you.

What you receive, and privacy

Not for you? Just tell them. No hard feelings, and they'll find someone else to fear.
