Switch on Ironclad alerts.

For the person running Ironclad only. About ten minutes, once. After this, alerts work for everyone who uses the app, and nobody else ever sees this page.

🏋️Every user's referee gets their check-ins, with photos on Android
⚖️Weekly verdicts go out on schedule, even if someone goes quiet
🔒Each user gets their own private space, locked with their own key

Easiest on a computer, but a phone works. Keep this page open in one tab and Cloudflare in another. The free plan comfortably handles a few dozen active users.

1

Make a free Cloudflare account

Go to dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up and sign up. The free plan is plenty. No card needed.

2

Create the server

  1. In the left menu, open Workers & Pages, then tap Create.
  2. Choose to start from Hello World. Name it ironclad and tap Deploy.
  3. Tap Edit code. Select everything in the editor, delete it, and paste the server code.
  4. Tap Deploy.

3

Give it storage

  1. In the left menu, open Storage & Databases → KV and tap Create. Name it ironclad.
  2. Go back to your ironclad Worker. Open Settings → Bindings, tap Add, and pick KV namespace.
  3. Set the variable name to STATE, choose the ironclad namespace, and save.

The name must be exactly STATE, in capitals.

4

Turn on the hourly check

Still in the Worker's Settings, find Triggers → Cron Triggers, tap Add, and enter 0 * * * * (every hour). This is what sends the weekly verdict on time.

5

Copy the server's address

Open your Worker's address. It's shown at the top of the Worker's page and ends in .workers.dev. Your server page appears with a checklist; everything should be ticked.

Tap Copy the address. That's the only thing the app needs. It's built into the app once, and from then on Ironclad alerts switch on for everyone.

The address isn't secret. Every app gets its own private key automatically when someone turns alerts on.

If something's off

  • The server page says "Almost there": the storage binding is missing or not named STATE (step 3).
  • A user's Today screen says "alerts off": their referee agreed but hasn't tapped Turn on alerts. They should resend the link.
  • Lots of people sign up at once: sign-ups are capped at 200 a day and 5 an hour from one network, to protect the free plan.
  • No weekly verdict arrived: check that the checklist on your server page shows the hourly check ran.
  • An iPhone referee sees no Turn on alerts button: they need iOS 16.4 or later, and must open Ironclad from the Home Screen, not Safari.
