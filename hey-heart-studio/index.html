The scene library

Every scene is written and signed off by a paediatrician

The AI's job is narrow: match what was said in the room to scenes that already exist, then personalise the name and the plan. That keeps every video accurate, on-brand and ready in seconds.

Approved, not generated

Medical content is written once, checked by clinicians and version-controlled. The AI never invents an explanation.

Personal where it helps

The child's name, what the doctor actually said, the questions they asked, and the family's own plan.

Clinician in the loop

One-tap sign-off before anything is sent. Every edit is logged against the library version.
