# Hey Lungs: The Wheezy Whistle (asthma and puffers)

Demo child: **Leo**, age 5. Doctor: **Dr Sarah**. Narrator: Elizabeth (as "your lungs").
Same world rules as the heart story: one boy, his bedroom, one afternoon to bedtime; felt-and-clay stop-motion.

## Characters

- **Leo**: about 5, felt-and-clay puppet, curly brown yarn hair, green-and-white striped t-shirt, navy overalls, red sneakers. Five fingers on each hand.
- **The Lungs**: two soft pale-pink felt "pillow" lungs joined at the top by a little windpipe "trunk", big googly eyes on each, small and friendly. When they're grumpy their cheeks puff and they wheeze a tiny whistle.
- **Blue Puffer** (reliever) and **Spacer**: real-looking, not cartoon faces, so children recognise their own.

## The mystery

A whistly sound ("wheeee") has been coming from Leo's chest. The Lungs want to find out what makes the whistle and what makes it go away. Clue tracker: **Tubes? → Squeeze? → Triggers? → Helper! → Tunnel!**

## Quiz (3 questions)

1. What do your lungs help you do? **Breathe** / Eat / Sleep
2. Which puffer helps fast when your tubes are squeezy? **The blue one** / Any toy / Nothing
3. If breathing is hard, what do you do? **Tell a grown-up straight away** / Hide / Keep running

## Versions by age (like the heart story)

- **Little (3–4):** shorter lines; skip the goo and triggers detail; keep the flower and candle breathing and the blue puffer as "the helper".
- **Big (7–10):** use the words *airways*, *muscles tighten*, *swelling*, *mucus*, *reliever* and *preventer*, triggers including exercise; spacer technique "shake, 1 puff, 4 breaths, repeat as your plan says"; Asthma Action Plan; when to call an ambulance (000) is for the parent page.

## Clinical checks for Dr Sarah before production

- Spacer wording follows the common "1 puff, 4 breaths" teaching. Number of puffs is left to the child's own action plan.
- Scene 8 (preventer) is optional per child.
- Emergency guidance for parents (ambulance 000, the 4×4×4 first-aid steps) belongs on the parent summary, not in the child's story.

## Production estimate

13 scenes: about 12 new AI shots (most 5–8 s), narration in one take, and the same app.

- **Everygen (Kling 3.0 Pro):** about 13–21 credits per shot, so about 250 credits in total. This is by far the cheapest route.
- **ElevenLabs video:** about 3,400 credits per 5 s shot, so about 45,000–60,000 credits in total, plus about 1,500 for narration.
