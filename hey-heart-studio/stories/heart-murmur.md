# Hey Heart: What makes my sounds? (innocent murmur)

Demo child: **Mia**, age 5. Doctor: **Dr Sarah**. Narrator: Elizabeth (as "your heart").

The mystery: the heart makes three sounds, **lub**, **whoosh** and **dub**. We find out what makes each one.
Sound tracker on screen: **Lub? Dub? Whoosh?** → **Lub ✓ Dub ✓** (dolls house) → **Found the whoosh!**
