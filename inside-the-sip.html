The Sip

Inside the Sip

Take one sip of a sugary drink — then travel inside your own body to watch what it does. Feel the enamel weaken, the blood sugar spike, insulin scramble to respond, the dopamine rush and crash, and see how doing this again and again drives fat storage and heart risk.

Best in the Meta Quest browser — tap “Begin”, then the green Enter VR button. Works on a computer or phone too (drag to look around). ~90 seconds. An educational visualisation, not medical advice.
