The little guy who stops you scrolling

Open a social media app and Mr Blocky toddles across the top of your screen, boops the close button with his nose, and shakes his head at you.

How it works

Pick the apps or websites that eat your time. Mr Blocky keeps watch, and steps in when you open one.

1You open the app

A moment later, Mr Blocky walks in and heads for the close button.

2He closes it

Boop. The screen goes dark, he shakes his head, and you are back on your home screen.

3Sometimes, a deal

Now and then he goes quiet… then asks if you want to dance with him.

Dance your way back in

Say yes and the lights come up. He calls out each move for a minute and you copy him. Make it to the end and he boops the app back open, and leaves you be for 5 minutes.

  • Side to side
  • Squats
  • Star jumps
  • Knees up
  • Arms up and wiggle
  • Spin around
  • Ta-da!

It's a little workout too. On a phone, hold on to it: it counts your squats, star jumps and knees up.

It comes with its own song, so turn your sound up.

You're in charge

Choose what he closes

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Reddit and others are ticked to start with. Add or remove any you like.

Take a break

Really need to get in? Let yourself in for 5 minutes, any time.

Dance deals, or not

He offers one about one time in four, never twice in two hours. Turn them off if you'd rather he just said no.

See your progress

He keeps count of how many times he's closed something for you, today and all time.

Questions

Why does the Android app need "Usage access"?

So it can tell which app you've just opened. That's checked on your phone and never sent anywhere.

And "Display over other apps"?

That's what lets Mr Blocky walk across the top of the app you opened.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Can I still use the apps?

Yes. Switch him off, take a 5-minute break, or dance with him when he offers. He's there to make you stop and think, not to lock you out.

Does he work on websites too?

Yes, with the browser extension. When he closes a website, he closes the tab.
