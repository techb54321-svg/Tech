Mr Blocky

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Mr Blocky does not collect, keep or share any personal information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Everything he needs to do his job stays on your own device.

This policy covers the Mr Blocky app for Android and the Mr Blocky browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox.

The Android app

The app has no permission to use the internet, so it has no way to send anything anywhere. It asks for these permissions, and uses them only on your phone:

  • Usage access, to tell which app you have just opened, so that Mr Blocky can step in when it is one on your list. The app shows the last few apps it noticed on its own screen, to help if something goes wrong, and forgets them when it closes.
  • Display over other apps, to draw Mr Blocky on top of the app you opened.
  • Notifications, for the "Mr Blocky is on duty" notification that Android requires while he keeps watch.
  • Run at start-up, so that he goes back on duty after your phone restarts.
  • The motion sensor, only during a dance, to count your squats, star jumps and knees up. The readings are used there and then, and not kept.

The app keeps your settings (which apps he closes, whether he is switched on, breaks and dance deals) and a count of how many times he has closed something, on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you use Android's own backup, your phone may include these settings in your backup, which is stored by Google under your account, not by us.

The browser extension

The extension runs on the websites you visit, to check whether each one is on your list of sites. It does not record or send anywhere which sites you visit or what is on them.

  • Your settings (your list of sites, whether he is switched on, snoozes and dance deals) are kept in your browser's own storage. If you have switched on sync in your browser, your browser may copy these settings to your other devices through your browser account. That happens between you and your browser's maker (such as Google, Mozilla or Microsoft); we never see them.
  • A count of how many tabs he has closed, and the time a dance pass runs out, are kept in your browser on that device only.
  • The dance music is a file inside the extension. The extension does not download anything or contact any server.

Children

Mr Blocky is not directed at children under 13, and collects no information from anyone, of any age.
