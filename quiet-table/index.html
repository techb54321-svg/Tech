        What the labels mean

          Where the ratings come from

          Diners like you. After a meal, tap Rate the noise and answer two quick questions. Your phone can also measure the sound for 10 seconds.

          Photos and reviews. Before anyone has rated a place, we look at its photos and reviews. Carpets, tablecloths, booths, curtains and acoustic panels make a room quieter. Hard floors, high bare ceilings, a big bar or TVs make it louder. These show as “Likely quiet” with a dashed outline, and real diner ratings always come first.

          Tips for a quieter meal

          • Go at lunchtime or early evening. Friday and Saturday nights are the loudest.
          • Ask for a corner table or a booth, away from the kitchen and the bar.
          • Sit with your back to the wall so sound comes from in front of you.
