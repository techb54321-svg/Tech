      How Quiet Table works

      Finding places. Tell us where you are, how far you'll go, and what you'd like to eat. We list the restaurants and cafés nearby.

      Quiet ratings come from diners like you. After a meal, tap “Rate the noise” and answer two quick questions. You can also let your phone measure the noise for 10 seconds.

      What the labels mean:

      • Quiet Easy conversation, soft or no music.
      • Fairly quiet Conversation takes a little effort.
      • Noisy You'll need to raise your voice.
      • Very noisy Hard to hear each other.
      • Not rated yet No one has rated it. Be the first!

      Tip: Most places are quieter at lunchtime and early evening than on Friday and Saturday nights. Ask for a corner table or a booth, away from the kitchen and the bar.

      Restaurant details come from OpenStreetMap contributors.
