Our story

It started with a rescue named Rosie.

Before she was two years old, Rosie had already lived in six homes. She came to us from a shelter — matted, sore, covered in skin infections, and shut down. She'd been left in a front yard like an ornament: no walks, no warmth, no one to belong to. And when no one wanted her anymore, she was dumped.

The day I brought her home, I made her a promise: from here on, your life is going to be wonderful.

Rosie holds onto that promise tight. She sticks to me like glue — as if she's making up for all the love she went without. But when I leave the house, the old fear finds her again. She howls at the door, sure she's been left behind for good. It isn't bad behaviour. It's a dog who is only just learning that love can stay.

So I went looking for something to help her feel calmer in those moments — and I couldn't find it. Everything was either medication, or a tub of preservatives and fillers dressed up as "calming." I didn't want to sedate her. I just wanted something gentle and honest: real, healthy, minimal ingredients that could take the edge off and help her feel safe.

When I couldn't find it, I made it. For Rosie first — and now for every dog like her.

Six homes before she was two. This is the one that stayed.

Questions, honestly answered

Will it sedate my dog? +

No. Rosie Bear is made to help your dog settle and feel calmer — not to knock them out or dull their personality. They stay themselves, just a little more at ease.

Is it safe to give every day? +

It's made from simple, food-grade ingredients designed for regular use. As with any new treat, we'll always share clear feeding guidance, and it's wise to check with your vet if your dog has health conditions.

Does it actually work? +

It's designed to help take the edge off in stressful moments. Every dog is different, and calm builds best alongside routine, patience, and training — Rosie Bear is there to help, not to be a magic fix.

What if my dog really struggles on their own? +

For dogs who find being alone genuinely hard, a calming aid isn't the whole answer. We'd always encourage pairing it with a good trainer or behaviourist — Rosie Bear helps take the edge off while you do the deeper work together.
